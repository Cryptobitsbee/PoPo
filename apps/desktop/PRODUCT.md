# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Windows-based engineers, writers, and knowledge workers who dictate while staying inside the application and text field where they are already working. They need fast, dependable voice input without changing windows, moving focus, or managing transcripts manually.

## Product Purpose

popo is a system-level voice keyboard daemon. A global hotkey captures speech, transcribes it, optionally applies a reusable writing mode, and pastes the result at the existing cursor. Success means the interaction feels like quiet operating-system infrastructure: immediate, reliable, private, and nearly invisible.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, considered. popo should feel like an obsidian instrument for serious work: calm enough to disappear into the task, but refined enough to earn trust whenever it surfaces.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a marketing surface, generic enterprise SaaS, a decorative AI assistant, or a transcription notepad. Reject neon or purple AI gradients, gratuitous glassmorphism, card-heavy dashboards, microphone decoration, invented affordances, ornamental motion, and unfamiliar controls without a task-driven reason.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve the user’s flow: interaction should happen at the cursor with minimal attention shift.
2. Earn familiarity: standard product affordances and consistent behavior matter more than novelty.
3. Practice quiet authority: every visible element must carry information or enable an action.
4. Make state unmistakable: active, selected, disabled, processing, success, and error states must remain clear without decorative color.
5. Treat reliability and privacy as part of the interface, not implementation details.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support complete keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic names and states, readable contrast, and concise copy. Motion must communicate state rather than decorate, stay brief, and respect reduced-motion preferences where animation is present. Never rely on color alone to communicate status.
